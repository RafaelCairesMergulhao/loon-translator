# Privacy policy draft — legal review required

Loon Translator processes microphone and computer playback audio to provide
speech recognition, translation and speech synthesis.

In Local mode, audio, recognized text and generated speech remain on the
computer. Language packs are downloaded only when requested. In Hybrid or
Cloud mode, recognized text may be sent to the translation provider and
translated text may be sent to the selected speech provider. The interface
requests consent before first cloud use.

Conversation history is disabled by default. If enabled, it is stored locally
under `%APPDATA%\LoonTranslator`. Users can delete local history and synthesized
audio cache from the application. Technical logs do not contain transcript
content by default.

Loon Translator does not contain advertising or sell conversation data.

Before publication, the publisher must add its legal identity, contact details,
jurisdiction, retention periods, provider list, data-subject request process,
children's privacy position and region-specific disclosures such as GDPR,
LGPD and applicable US state laws.
