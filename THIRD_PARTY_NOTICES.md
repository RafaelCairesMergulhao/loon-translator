# Third-party notices

Loon Translator is distributed with open-source components. Before every
commercial release, regenerate and audit the complete dependency inventory
from the locked environment.

Core components include:

- Faster-Whisper — MIT
- CTranslate2 — MIT
- CustomTkinter — MIT
- Argos Translate engine — MIT / CC0
- deep-translator — MIT
- edge-tts — LGPL-3.0
- NumPy — BSD-3-Clause
- python-sounddevice — MIT
- PyAudioWPatch — MIT
- libsndfile/python-soundfile — BSD/LGPL components

The license of a translation or voice model is separate from the engine
license. Loon Translator does not bundle Argos or Piper model files in its base
distribution. Models downloaded by the user must display and preserve their
own license and attribution. Some public models have incomplete or restrictive
training-data terms and must not be marked as commercially approved without an
individual review.

VB-CABLE is third-party donationware from VB-Audio. It is not bundled with Loon
Translator. Users must obtain it from the official vendor.

Microsoft Windows voices are provided by the operating system and are not
redistributed by Loon Translator.

This document is an engineering inventory, not legal advice.
